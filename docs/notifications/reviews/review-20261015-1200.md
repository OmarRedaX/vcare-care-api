- [x] RESOLVED — Contacts-failure backoff escalates once per claimed batch, not per tick, so a short Identity blip parks rows for up to 30 min · `src/app/notifications/service/outbox-delivery.service.ts:47-51,90-104`
  - Scenario: 200 due rows, `OUTBOX_BATCH_SIZE=20`. Identity contacts is down for ~10 s. Round 1 delays its 20 rows by 30 s and does `contactFailures=1`. The delayed rows are no longer due, so round 2 claims the next 20, delays them by 60 s, and so on through 10 rounds (30 s, 60 s, ... 1800 s). Rows in later rounds are parked for 10 to 30 min after an outage that lasted seconds. Reminders for these rows can then hit `reminder_expired` or `consultation_changed` and be dropped.
  - Rule: spec N-D1 / N-R7 (a contacts failure only delays; delay grows per consecutive failed lookup) and integration.md "per consecutive failed batch".
  - Fix: after the first batch with `contacts.unavailable.size > 0`, stop the round loop for this tick (break from `tick`). Escalate `contactFailures` once per tick and reset it on a clean lookup. The fix needs a way for `deliverRound` to report the outage, for example a `contactsUnavailable` flag in `DeliveryRoundResult`.
  - Test gap: no test with more than one full batch where Identity contacts fails. Assert a single backoff step and that later rows stay due.
  - RESOLVED: `DeliveryRoundResult.contactsUnavailable`; `tick` breaks after the first batch whose lookup failed, so `contactFailures` rises once per tick and resets on a clean lookup; later rows stay due (`src/app/notifications/service/outbox-delivery.service.ts:50-53`, `prepare`). Spec N-D1 / N-R7 / 3.4 updated first. Tests: unit "should stop the tick after the first batch whose contacts lookup failed" and integration "should take one backoff step per tick ... several batches".

- [x] RESOLVED — Lease is not tied to `OUTBOX_BATCH_SIZE`; a large batch is re-claimed mid-batch and sends duplicate emails · `src/app/notifications/constants.ts:12-15`, `src/lib/config/env.ts:125`
  - Scenario: `OUTBOX_BATCH_SIZE=100` (allowed maximum) with a slow provider at the 5 s send timeout gives up to 500 s of sequential sends against a 300 s lease. After 300 s the unsent rows become due and another worker or the next tick claims them (`attempts+1`). The first worker's later send still goes out, then its guarded finish fails as `lease_lost`. The recipient gets the email twice. The default of 20 (100 s) is safe, so this needs misconfiguration plus a slow provider.
  - Rule: spec N-D3 (lease must exceed the worst-case batch) and the lease comment in `constants.ts`.
  - Fix: set `.max(55)` in env.ts, or derive the lease as `batch * EMAIL_SEND_TIMEOUT_MS / 1000 + margin`. Optionally stop sending new rows when the elapsed time exceeds the lease.
  - Test gap: no env-bound test or lease-vs-batch invariant test.
  - RESOLVED: `OUTBOX_BATCH_SIZE` max 59 (`src/lib/config/env.ts`), `.env.example`, infrastructure.md and spec 3.9 / N-D12 updated; tests: env bound (59 ok, 60 rejected) and a lease invariant test (`tests/unit/app/notifications/outbox-lease.test.ts`).

  - Test gap: no test of a DB failure between claim and finish.

Spec N-D10 / 3.4 step 8. Tests: three unit tests and an integration test.
